How to play
  1. Tap a tube to pick up its top color, then tap another tube to pour.
  2. You can only pour onto the same color or into an empty tube, and only while there's room.
  3. Fill every tube with a single color to win.
  4. Beat par (the solver's move count) for ★★★ and bonus coins.
  5. Mystery levels hide blocks under ? until they reach the top.
  6. Stuck? Undo is free. Hints and extra tubes cost coins.

Keys: 1–9, 0 select tubes · Z undo · R restart · H hint
